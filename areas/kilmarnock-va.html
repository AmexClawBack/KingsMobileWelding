FAQ

Mobile welding in Kilmarnock, VA.

Do you provide mobile welding in Kilmarnock, Virginia?

Yes. Kings Mobile Welding supports qualifying contractor, commercial, industrial, infrastructure and repair scopes in Kilmarnock, Lancaster County and surrounding areas of Virginia's Northern Neck.

What types of welding work can you perform?

Common scopes include structural steel welding, field modifications, engineered repairs, miscellaneous metals, industrial welding, pile and deep foundation welding, marine and waterfront welding, and contractor-directed field fabrication.

Are your welders AWS certified?

Kings Mobile Welding provides AWS D1.1 structural welding and AWS D1.5 bridge welding capability for applicable project requirements.

Can you work from engineered drawings or repair details?

Yes. We routinely review project drawings, weld details, specifications and contractor-provided repair or reinforcement information before performing field work. Engineering design and approval remain with the project's responsible engineer or authority when required.

What should I send to request pricing?

Send the project location, drawings or repair details, photos, quantities, access conditions, inspection requirements, desired schedule and any applicable welding specifications. That information helps us review the scope before mobilization.
