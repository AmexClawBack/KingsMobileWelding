A good mobile welding contractor should do more than show up with a machine. The crew needs to understand access, hot work rules, fire watch, lifts, material prep, fit-up, drawings, field conditions, safety requirements, inspection expectations and schedule pressure.

For commercial and industrial clients, this often means supporting work that cannot easily be removed and taken to a shop. What to Send for a Mobile Welding Quote

The fastest way to get pricing is to send enough information to understand the scope, access and safety requirements before the truck rolls.

  • Project address, site contact and target date
  • Photos and videos of the damaged steel, connection, gate, equipment or repair area
  • Drawings, sketches, dimensions, material thickness and member sizes when available
  • Whether the work is commercial, industrial, structural, emergency, bridge/DOT, marine or property repair
  • Whether AWS D1.1, AWS D1.5, special inspection, CWI, NDT, WPS or documentation is required
  • Access method: drive-up, ladder, lift, scaffold, roof access, indoor access, shutdown window or restricted area
  • Safety requirements: hot work permit, fire watch, fall protection, ventilation, lockout/tagout or traffic control
