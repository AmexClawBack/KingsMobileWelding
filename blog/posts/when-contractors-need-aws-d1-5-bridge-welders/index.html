What Is AWS D1.5 Bridge Welding?

AWS D1.5 is commonly referenced for welding on steel bridge work. For contractors, the practical point is simple: if the project specifications, DOT requirements, engineer, inspector, or owner require AWS D1.5, the welding crew, procedure requirements, documentation, inspection plan, and field execution need to match that requirement.

Not every structural welding job is a bridge welding job. A welder may be qualified for AWS D1.1 structural work and still need specific bridge welding qualifications, project approval, WPS requirements, or documentation depending on the scope.

DOT and Infrastructure Welding Requirements

DOT and public works projects can involve additional submittals, documentation, access coordination, safety requirements, inspection hold points, lane closures, night work, and project-specific welding requirements. That means the welding crew should be brought into the planning early enough to review the details, not after the closure window has already started.

Field Conditions That Make Bridge Welding Different

  • Limited access under or around bridge members
  • Night work, traffic control, and lane closure timing
  • Weather exposure, wind, temperature, and surface preparation challenges
  • Existing coatings, corrosion, damaged steel, or tight repair areas
  • Inspector hold points before fit-up, during welding, or after final welds
  • Coordination with cranes, lifts, rigging, concrete work, demolition, or other trades

AWS D1.5 Bridge Welding FAQ

When do contractors need AWS D1.5 bridge welders?

Contractors commonly need AWS D1.5 bridge welders when the scope involves bridge steel, DOT bridge components, expansion joints, diaphragms, cross frames, stiffeners, girder repairs, bearing areas, bridge pile work, or project specifications that require bridge welding qualifications.

Is every structural welder qualified for bridge welding?

No. Structural welding experience is not automatically the same as bridge welding qualification. When a bridge project specification requires AWS D1.5, the contractor should confirm required welder qualifications, WPS requirements, inspection requirements, and documentation before work starts.

Can bridge welding be performed during night work?

Yes, when the project is planned for night work or lane closure windows. The welding crew needs access, lighting, power, hot work controls, traffic control coordination, inspection timing, and a clear production plan.

What should I send for pricing?

Send drawings, bridge component details, weld symbols, material information, project specifications, WPS requirements, inspection or NDT requirements, access method, traffic control or night work restrictions, schedule, and photos of the work area.
