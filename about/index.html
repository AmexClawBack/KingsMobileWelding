About / Kings Mobile Welding

Built for demanding field work.

Kings Mobile Welding is a Virginia-based field welding and fabrication contractor supporting construction, infrastructure, deep foundation, industrial, and structural steel projects throughout the Mid-Atlantic.

What We're Building For

Reliable field execution that supports the entire project.

Our role is to provide contractors and project teams with qualified welding support that fits the realities of active construction. That means more than producing a sound weld. It means understanding access, sequencing, inspection requirements, safety controls, equipment coordination, documentation, and the schedule surrounding the work.

Purpose Support critical construction work with qualified, dependable field welding.
Mission Make welding scopes easier for contractors to plan, coordinate, inspect, and complete.
Our Foundation

Built from field experience.

Kings Mobile Welding was established around a straightforward idea: contractors need welding partners who understand the jobsite as well as the weld itself.

The company's foundation is hands-on experience across welding, fabrication, structural steel, industrial work, and construction environments. That background continues to shape how Kings approaches communication, preparation, fit-up, safety, inspection, and field coordination.

How We Operate

Field execution comes first.

01

Qualified Work

AWS D1.1 structural and AWS D1.5 bridge capability for applicable inspection-driven scopes, with NCDOT bridge-certified welders available for qualifying work.

02

Project Coordination

Direct coordination with estimators, project managers, superintendents, inspectors, field supervision, and other trades when the welding scope affects sequencing or access.

03

Field Readiness

Mobile welding and field fabrication capability prepared for active jobsites, changing conditions, restricted access, and schedule-driven construction work.

04

Documentation

Insurance, COI, W-9, certification information, safety documentation, SWaM details, UEI, CAGE, and onboarding information available when required.
