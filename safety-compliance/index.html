How We Work

Field work starts with the work plan.

Before welding begins, we review the scope, access, surrounding work, project requirements, and conditions that affect safe execution. Our crews coordinate with site leadership around hot work, access, staging, fire watch requirements, equipment, and inspection points before the work moves forward.

01
Pre-Task Planning

Confirm the conditions before the work starts.

Field welding depends on more than the weld detail. Access, surrounding trades, equipment, inspection points, and site restrictions all affect how the work needs to be planned.

  • Scope and drawing review
  • Site hazards and surrounding work
  • Access and staging requirements
  • Required PPE and site rules
  • Lift, scaffold, or crane needs
  • Inspection hold points
  • Hot-work permit requirements
  • Sequencing with other trades
02
Area Control

Set up the work area for the conditions on site.

Cutting, grinding, gouging, and welding create hazards that have to be controlled around the actual project environment. We coordinate work-area setup with the controlling contractor or facility before hot work begins.

  • Spark and slag control
  • Combustible-material awareness
  • Fire watch coordination when required
  • Barricades and work-area control
  • Finished-surface protection
  • Housekeeping and safe teardown
Hot Work & Field Conditions

Safety has to fit the jobsite.

Mobile welding may take place around active crews, occupied facilities, operating plants, traffic, finished surfaces, equipment, and other trades. The work plan has to account for the environment around the weld as well as the welding itself.

Hot WorkPermit procedures, fire watch requirements, spark control, and work-area protection.
AccessLifts, scaffolding, ladders, barges, cranes, forklifts, and restricted work areas.
CoordinationSuperintendents, foremen, safety teams, inspectors, facility personnel, and other trades.
CloseoutHousekeeping, punch work, inspection points, photos, and documentation when required.

FAQ

Safety & qualification questions.

Are you fully insured?

Yes. Kings Mobile Welding is fully insured and can provide a certificate of insurance for contractor onboarding, procurement, project setup, or mobilization.

Can you provide welding qualification documentation?

Yes. Welding qualification and certification information can be provided when required for the scope, inspection plan, or contractor onboarding process.

Can you work under our site-specific safety program?

Yes. We coordinate with project leadership and follow the safety, access, orientation, permit, hot-work, and documentation procedures established for the site.

What should we send for safety or prequalification review?

Send the project safety requirements, onboarding forms, required documents, project location, anticipated schedule, and welding scope through our contact page.

Prequalifying Kings for a project?

Send the project safety requirements, onboarding forms, required documentation, project location, anticipated schedule, and welding scope. We’ll review the package and provide the information needed for prequalification or mobilization.
