Where We Work

Welding support for the steel that keeps power projects moving.

Power infrastructure includes far more than one type of structure. A utility project can involve transmission poles, lattice towers, substations, equipment support steel, access platforms, utility frames, solar racking, BESS support steel, structural repairs, and commissioning punch-list items — often under the same EPC or prime contractor.

Kings works inside that larger project as a field-welding subcontractor. We support defined structural and mechanical welding scopes while the utility, EPC, electrical contractor, civil contractor, steel team, or owner retains responsibility for engineering, electrical work, energized operations, and overall project control.

Solar EPC & BESS

Field welding for the problems that show up after the drawings hit the site.

Solar and battery-storage projects are repetitive by design, but field conditions are not always repetitive. Damaged posts, racking conflicts, support-steel changes, bracket repairs, equipment-support issues, and commissioning punch-list items can create small structural scopes that still need qualified welding and fast coordination.

Solar Racking Damaged racking members, tabs, plates, brackets, gussets, tracker-support components, and contractor-approved field corrections where welding is permitted by the project requirements.
Torque Tubes / Trackers Repair or reinforcement support where the manufacturer, engineer, or project team provides an approved repair detail and allows field welding.
Piles & Posts Localized repair, reinforcement, caps, support steel, damaged post corrections, or other structural welding tied to the project design.
BESS Support Steel Container-related support steel, brackets, equipment supports, guards, bollards, access steel, platforms, and field modifications.
Commissioning Punch List Late-stage repair welding, missing attachments, failed weld corrections, structural modifications, and rework that needs to be closed before turnover.
Scope Boundary

Structural and mechanical welding — not energized electrical work.

Power projects can put several trades inside the same work area. Kings handles the field-welding portion while the utility, EPC, or electrical contractor controls the electrical system and site-specific energized-work requirements.

01
Kings Scope

Structural steel welding, cutting, fit-up, field fabrication, reinforcement, repairs, support steel, utility structure modifications, and contractor-directed structural work.

02
Engineering

Engineering design, repair design, structural calculations, and final approval remain with the responsible engineer, owner, utility, EPC, manufacturer, or other authorized design party.

03
Electrical Work

Energized work, live-line work, switching, grounding, conductor work, terminations, commissioning, and other electrical responsibilities remain with the utility or electrical contractor.

04
Repair Execution

When an engineered or manufacturer-approved repair is required, Kings performs the welding to the approved detail, material requirements, weld symbols, and applicable WPS.

Project Support

Field work that fits the construction sequence.

Utility-scale projects are schedule-driven. Welding often happens after a field conflict, inspection item, damaged component, revised detail, outage window, or work area becomes available. The crew has to fit into that sequence without slowing down the rest of the project.

04

Cutting, fit-up, reinforcement, repair welding, support steel, field fabrication, structural modifications, installation support, and inspection coordination.

05

FAQ

Power infrastructure welding questions.

Do you support transmission and substation projects?

Yes. Kings supports qualifying structural field-welding scopes involving transmission poles and towers, steel transmission pole repair, lattice tower repair, substation structural steel, utility structures, reinforcement, weathering steel, field corrections, and short-notice utility repair work.

Do you support solar EPC and BESS projects?

Yes. We support defined structural welding and repair scopes involving racking and tracker components, posts, support steel, equipment-related steel, guards, bollards, platforms, brackets, and commissioning punch-list work where field welding is permitted by the project requirements.

Do you perform energized or live-line electrical work?

No. Our role is structural and mechanical field welding and fabrication. Electrical, energized, live-line, switching, grounding, terminations, commissioning, and conductor-related responsibilities remain with the utility or electrical contractor.

Can you mobilize for emergency utility repairs?

Yes, for qualifying short-notice structural welding scopes when the work area is safe, the repair direction has been established, and the required access, materials, welding procedure, and project controls are available.

Have a power infrastructure scope to review?

Send the drawings, photos, repair details, project location, material information, welding requirements, access conditions, commissioning or outage schedule, and inspection requirements. We’ll review the field-welding scope and confirm availability.
