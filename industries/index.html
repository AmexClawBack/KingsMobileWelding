Deep Foundations

Built to work alongside pile driving and foundation crews.

Deep foundation work places welding directly in the production path. Kings supports pile driving contractors with field welding for driven pile, casing, temporary works, and foundation-related steel where fit-up, access, sequencing, and inspection can directly affect progress.

Driven Foundations

  • H-pile splices and extensions
  • Pipe pile splice welding
  • Pile caps and bracing
  • Field fit-up and modifications

Foundation & Temporary Works

  • Permanent and drilled shaft casing
  • Sheet pile welding
  • Cofferdams
  • Walers, struts, and bracing
