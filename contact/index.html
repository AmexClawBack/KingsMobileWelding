Before You Send

The more we know, the faster we can review it.

These details help us understand the scope, plan access and equipment, and determine what questions need to be answered before pricing or scheduling.

01

Drawings / Photos

Plans, weld details, repair photos, marked-up drawings, specifications, sketches, or measurements.

02

Location

City and state, project name, facility, bridge, site address, and any access or gate information.

03

Scope

What needs to be welded, repaired, installed, reinforced, modified, fabricated, or replaced.

04

Access

Height, lift requirements, crane or forklift access, barge access, restricted spaces, or lane closures.

05

Requirements

AWS D1.1, AWS D1.5, DOT, owner, inspection, hot-work, or project-specific qualification requirements.

06

Schedule

Bid date, requested mobilization, shutdown window, milestone, emergency need, or required completion date.

FAQ

Project request questions.

Can you review drawings before visiting the site?

Yes. Drawings, photos, marked-up plans, repair details, and measurements can help us review the scope and identify questions before a site visit or mobilization.

Can I request vendor or prequalification documents here?

Yes. You can use the form for a vendor or prequalification request, but our Procurement page has the company identifiers and onboarding information most contractor teams need.

Do you travel for projects?

Yes. Kings Mobile Welding supports qualified work throughout Virginia and the Mid-Atlantic, with traveling project support available depending on the scope, schedule, access, and project requirements.
