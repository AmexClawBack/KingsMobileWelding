FAQ

Vendor setup questions.

Can you provide a W-9 and certificate of insurance?

Yes. A W-9 and certificate of insurance are available for vendor setup, subcontractor onboarding, and project review.

Are you registered in SAM.gov and Virginia eVA?

Yes. Kings Mobile Welding is registered in SAM.gov and is a Virginia eVA vendor. UEI, CAGE, eVA, and related vendor information can be provided for onboarding.

Are you Virginia SWaM certified?

Yes. Kings Mobile Welding is Virginia SWaM certified. Certification number: 838033.

What should a prime contractor send for vendor onboarding?

Send the vendor packet, subcontractor onboarding forms, requested documentation, project location, anticipated schedule, and welding scope through our contact page.

Adding Kings to your vendor list?

Send your vendor packet, subcontractor onboarding forms, project requirements, or requested documentation. We’ll provide the company, insurance, qualification, and procurement information needed for review.
