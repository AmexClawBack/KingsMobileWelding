Deep Foundation Support

Built for pile driving and foundation work.

Deep foundation welding often means repetitive production, tight sequencing, heavy material, difficult access, and inspection requirements. Kings supports pile contractors with field welding capability organized around driven pile, casing, and temporary works.

Driven Pile

  • H-pile splices and extensions
  • Pipe pile splices and extensions
  • Large-diameter pile welding
  • Pile caps, bracing, and attachments
  • Field fit-up and alignment support

Foundation & Temporary Works

  • Permanent casing splices
  • Drilled shaft casing
  • Sheet pile welding
  • Cofferdam welding
  • Walers, struts, braces, and tie-ins

Project Support

From RFQ to field execution.

The welding scope is easier to manage when the right information is reviewed early. Our process is built around understanding the work, qualifications, access, equipment, and schedule before mobilization.

01

Scope Review

Drawings, specifications, photos, project location, anticipated schedule, access conditions, and manpower needs.

02

Qualification Review

Applicable welding code, welder qualification requirements, WPS/PQR expectations, inspection points, and documentation needs.

03

Mobilization Planning

Crew size, welding equipment, lifts, access, hot-work requirements, travel, sequencing, and site logistics.

04

Field Execution

Fit-up, cutting, preparation, welding, modification, coordination, and production around the active project.

05

Inspection & Closeout

Inspection support and requested project documentation as required by the scope and contracting team.
