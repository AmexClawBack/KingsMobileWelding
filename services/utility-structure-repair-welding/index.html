Utility Structures

Field welding for the structural steel around the utility system.

Not every utility repair fits neatly into a transmission-pole, tower, or substation category. Utility facilities and infrastructure include support frames, platforms, equipment steel, brackets, base connections, access structures, miscellaneous framing, and other structural components that may need reinforcement, repair, replacement, or field modification. Kings Mobile Welding provides the welding crew to execute those scopes in accordance with the project drawings, approved repair details, and applicable WPS requirements.

Structures We Support

Structural and miscellaneous steel across utility facilities.

This page is intentionally broad. It covers utility-related structural repair work that does not belong exclusively to a transmission pole, lattice tower, or substation service page.

01 / Frames & Supports

Support frames, structural steel & equipment-related framing.

Utility structures often include frames and secondary support steel around equipment, access systems, cable infrastructure, controls, or facility components. Kings can support repair, reinforcement, modification, or replacement welding where the project team has defined the required structural work.

  • Structural support frames
  • Equipment support steel
  • Secondary framing
  • Support beams and columns
  • Field-fabricated steel
  • Connection modifications
02 / Platforms & Access Steel

Platforms, stairs, handrails & maintenance access.

Existing utility facilities may need access steel repaired, modified, reinforced, or added around equipment and operating constraints. Kings supports structural welding for platform framing, stairs, handrails, guardrail, fixed access steel, and related miscellaneous metals.

  • Maintenance platforms
  • Access platforms
  • Structural stairs
  • Handrail and guardrail
  • Fixed access steel
  • Walkway framing
03 / Brackets & Attachments

Brackets, plates, supports & structural attachments.

Utility repair scopes frequently involve smaller structural components that are critical to equipment support, access, alignment, or protection. We can fabricate, modify, reinforce, or repair contractor-directed bracket and attachment steel in the field.

  • Structural brackets
  • Attachment plates
  • Mounting steel

  • Reinforcement plates

04 / Base Connections

Base plates, support connections & localized reinforcement.

Repairs near structural bases can involve base plates, stiffeners, connection steel, cracked welds, localized corrosion, reinforcement, or modifications required to correct an existing condition. Kings performs the structural welding to the approved repair or modification detail.

Repair & Reinforcement

When existing utility steel needs to be repaired instead of replaced.

Damage, deterioration, misalignment, changed equipment, inspection findings, and field conflicts can all create structural repair scopes. Where the project team elects to retain the existing structure, reinforcement and corrective welding may be used to restore or modify the steel to the approved condition.

Damaged Members Localized bending, deformation, impact damage, cracked attachments, damaged framing, or other structural conditions identified by the project team.
Reinforcement Plates, stiffeners, supplemental members, connection steel, brackets, or other strengthening components shown on approved repair details.
Weld Remediation Remove and repair cracked, deficient, incomplete, or damaged welds using grinding, carbon arc gouging, preparation, and rewelding where directed.
Field Corrections Modify steel that does not fit, conflicts with the actual field condition, needs relocation, or requires contractor-approved correction after installation.
Replacement Steel Cut out and replace localized structural members, brackets, plates, supports, or attachments where the approved repair calls for new steel.
Engineered Repair Execution

The project team defines the repair. Kings executes the welding.

Kings Mobile Welding does not independently engineer or approve utility structure repairs. The responsible engineer, utility, owner, EPC, or project design team provides or approves the repair detail, reinforcement requirements, connection configuration, and structural modifications.

Our role is field execution: preparation, fit-up, welding, reinforcement, corrective work, and closeout performed to the approved repair package and applicable WPS.

01
Repair Detail

Issued repair drawing, field directive, approved sketch, RFI response, weld symbols, dimensions, member details, and limits of work.

02
Material Requirements

Existing base metal, reinforcement steel, replacement member grade, plate thickness, filler-metal requirements, and surface condition.

03
WPS & Qualification

Applicable welding code, qualified procedure, welder requirements, low-hydrogen controls, preheat/interpass requirements, and inspection points.

04
Inspection & Closeout

Coordinate project hold points, owner or third-party inspection, requested field documentation, cleanup, and turnover requirements.

Field Capability

Repair work usually requires more than the final weld.

Existing utility structures often require preparation, removal, fitting, modification, and coordination before the structural weld can be completed.

01

Cutting & Removal

Field cutting, removal of damaged components, torch or plasma work, grinding, and preparation for replacement or reinforcement.

02

Carbon Arc Gouging

Remove defective weld metal, backgouge joints, prepare structural repairs, and open damaged areas where the approved repair requires it.

03

Fit-Up & Alignment

Position repair plates, members, brackets, and replacement steel to the approved dimensions, joint geometry, and field tolerances.

04

Field Fabrication

Cut, fit, modify, reinforce, and weld structural steel around actual field conditions when the project requires jobsite fabrication.

Existing Facilities

Built for repair work inside active utility environments.

Brownfield Modifications Existing structures, new equipment, revised access, changed layouts, replacement systems, or field conditions that require structural modification.
Active Operations Coordinate hot work, access, work-zone controls, adjacent operations, project sequencing, and site restrictions with the controlling contractor or utility.

What to Send

Give us the field condition, repair direction, and access information.

Utility structure repairs are easier to price and mobilize when the scope package includes enough information to understand the existing steel, the required repair, and how the crew will reach the work.

Drawings / Repair Detail Issued drawings, repair sketch, RFI response, weld symbols, member or plate dimensions, and drawing revision.
Photos Wide shots, close-ups, damaged areas, access conditions, adjacent equipment, member identification, and scale references.
Material Information Existing member grade if known, repair steel grade, thickness, quantity, and whether material is already on site.
Welding Requirements Applicable AWS code, WPS, filler-metal requirements, preheat/interpass controls, inspection requirements, and owner-specific requirements.
Access & Site Conditions Lifts, scaffold, excavation, rigging, work-at-height access, hot-work process, energized-area controls, weather, and work-zone restrictions.
Schedule Requested mobilization, outage or work window, number of structures, expected duration, working hours, and completion milestone.

FAQ

Utility structure repair questions.

What types of utility structures can Kings repair?

We support structural and miscellaneous steel associated with utility infrastructure, including support frames, equipment-related steel, platforms, brackets, stairs, handrails, base plates, access steel, damaged structural members, reinforcement, and contractor-directed field modifications.

Does Kings engineer utility structure repairs?

No. Repair design and structural approval remain with the responsible engineer, utility, owner, EPC, or project design team. Kings executes the field welding in accordance with approved drawings, repair details, material requirements, and applicable WPS requirements.

Do you perform energized electrical utility work?

No. Our scope is structural and mechanical field welding and fabrication. Electrical work, energized work, live-line work, switching, grounding, terminations, commissioning, and conductor-related responsibilities remain with the utility or electrical contractor.

Have a utility structure repair scope to review?

Send the drawings, photos, repair detail, material information, WPS requirements, access conditions, inspection requirements, project location, and schedule. We’ll review the structural welding scope and confirm availability.
