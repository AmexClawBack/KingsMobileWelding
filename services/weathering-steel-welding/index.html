Weathering Steel

The material matters before the first arc is struck.

Weathering steel is used on transmission structures, bridges, infrastructure, and other exposed structural applications. Field welding on existing weathering steel requires more than identifying the structure by appearance. The project team needs to confirm the base metal, repair material, joint detail, welding procedure, filler-metal requirements, preheat or interpass requirements, and inspection criteria before the repair is executed. Kings Mobile Welding provides the field-welding crew to perform that work to the approved requirements.

WPS-Controlled Work

Base metal, filler metal, and procedure have to work together.

The existing structure and the new repair steel are not always the same grade. A weathering-steel pole may receive reinforcement of another structural grade, or a bridge repair may use a project-specified plate material. The approved WPS and repair package should address how those materials are joined.

Base Metal Confirm the existing member grade, thickness, geometry, service condition, and whether the weld zone has coatings, corrosion products, or weathered scale that must be addressed.
Repair Material Confirm reinforcement or replacement steel grade, thickness, dimensions, joint preparation, fit-up requirements, and connection detail.
Filler Metal Use the electrode or filler-metal classification established by the approved WPS for the specific base-metal combination and project requirements.
Preheat / Interpass Follow the WPS for required preheat, interpass temperature, environmental limitations, electrode handling, and other procedure controls.
Inspection Coordinate visual inspection, hold points, owner or third-party inspection, and any project-specified NDT with the repair sequence.
Material & Procedure Controls

“Corten” is not enough information to weld from.

Field identification by color or appearance is not a substitute for confirming the actual material and repair requirements. Before welding, the project team should establish the base metal, reinforcement or replacement steel, joint configuration, weld size, welding sequence, and applicable WPS.

That information is especially important when existing weathering steel is being joined to a different structural grade or when the repair needs specific atmospheric-corrosion or strength characteristics.

01
Confirm the Grade

Use drawings, mill records, project documentation, owner information, or other approved identification methods to establish the existing material where required.

02
Confirm the Repair Steel

Reinforcement and replacement materials should match the engineer- or project-approved repair detail, not simply the dimensions of the damaged area.

03
Use the Approved WPS

The WPS controls the welding process, filler metal, preheat or interpass requirements, joint preparation, and other welding variables applicable to the repair.

04
Control the Field Conditions

Wind, moisture, temperature, surface contamination, electrode exposure, and access conditions can affect whether the repair can be welded in accordance with the procedure.

Field Execution

Procedure control has to survive the jobsite.

The approved WPS may be straightforward on paper, but the crew still has to apply it in real field conditions — around existing structures, access limitations, weather, excavation, elevation, and project sequencing.

01

Surface Preparation

Prepare the weld area as required by the repair detail and procedure, removing contamination, loose corrosion products, coatings, or unsuitable surface condition from the joint area.

02

Fit-Up

Position reinforcement or replacement steel to the approved geometry, gaps, alignment, and joint configuration before final welding begins.

03

Preheat Control

Apply and verify required preheat, maintain interpass limits, and coordinate the welding sequence around material thickness and project requirements.

04

Low-Hydrogen Practice

Handle electrodes and consumables in accordance with the applicable WPS and project controls for low-hydrogen structural welding.

05

Weld Sequence

Complete the welds in the order and progression established by the repair detail or WPS, including multi-pass, intermittent, or full-perimeter welds where specified.

06

Inspection & Cleanup

Support required inspection, complete final cleanup, and leave the repaired area ready for coating, weathering, restoration, or the next trade as directed by the project.

What to Send

Send enough information to confirm the material and procedure before mobilization.

Weathering-steel field work is easier to plan when the material information is included with the repair detail instead of being resolved after the welding crew reaches the site.

Existing Material Known material grade, thickness, member type, structure identification, mill information if available, and existing condition.
Repair Drawings Issued repair detail, weld symbols, reinforcement layout, dimensions, connection detail, and drawing revision.
Repair Steel Plate or member grade, thickness, dimensions, quantity, and whether material is supplied by the contractor or Kings.
WPS Requirements Applicable code, approved procedure, filler-metal requirements, preheat/interpass limits, electrode controls, and welding sequence.
Inspection Requirements Visual inspection, NDT, hold points, owner review, documentation requirements, and acceptance criteria.
Site Conditions Project location, access, elevation, excavation status, lifts or rigging, work windows, weather restrictions, and requested schedule.

FAQ

Weathering steel welding questions.

Can Kings weld ASTM A588 weathering steel?

Yes, for qualifying field-welding scopes where the project has established or approved the base metal, welding procedure, filler-metal requirements, preheat or interpass requirements, and inspection requirements applicable to the work.

Can you weld reinforcement plates to a weathering-steel transmission pole?

Yes, when the approved repair package identifies the existing pole material, reinforcement material, joint configuration, weld detail, filler-metal requirements, preheat/interpass controls, welding sequence, and applicable WPS.

Does Kings choose or engineer the weathering-steel repair procedure?

No. Repair design and structural approval remain with the responsible engineer, owner, utility, EPC, or project design team. Kings executes the field welding in accordance with the approved repair detail and applicable WPS.

Have a weathering-steel repair scope to review?

Send the material information, repair drawings, reinforcement or replacement steel details, WPS requirements, inspection requirements, field photos, site conditions, and requested schedule. We’ll review the welding scope and confirm availability.
