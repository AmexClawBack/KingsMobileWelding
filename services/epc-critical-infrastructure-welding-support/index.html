Welding Support for EPC Contractors

EPC contractors need field welding support that can fit into engineered project schedules, project drawings, construction sequencing, inspection requirements and coordination with other trades. Kings Mobile Welding supports defined welding scopes on engineering, procurement and construction projects as a welding subcontractor, not as the EPC contractor.

Support can include field installation welding, structural steel welding, field modifications, repair and reinforcement, project-specific connection welding, support steel, equipment support steel, punch-list welding and supplemental welding manpower for planned mobilization windows.

  • Work from project drawings, specifications and contractor direction
  • Support engineered repairs, modifications and field fit-up conditions
  • Coordinate with superintendents, PMs, estimators, inspectors and other trades
  • Provide supplemental welding manpower for scheduled construction phases

Critical Infrastructure Welding Services

Critical infrastructure projects often require reliable field welding support, documentation, coordination and the ability to work around project schedules, inspection hold points, access restrictions and other trades. Kings Mobile Welding provides welding services for critical infrastructure where the scope is contractor-directed and project requirements are provided.

Appropriate project environments can include transportation infrastructure, bridges, utilities, energy infrastructure, industrial facilities, ports and marine infrastructure, heavy civil construction, deep foundations, structural steel systems and public infrastructure.

  • Bridge, transportation and public infrastructure welding support
  • Utility and energy project welding support
  • Industrial facilities, ports, marine and heavy civil field welding
  • Deep foundation, pile, casing and foundation-related steel support

Welding Services for Energy & Utility Projects

Contractors searching for welding services for energy projects usually need jobsite welding support tied to a larger construction scope. Kings Mobile Welding can support utility-scale construction, energy infrastructure, structural steel, equipment support steel, field modifications, repairs, contractor punch-list work, scheduled project welding and shutdown or outage-related welding support where applicable.

We do not represent this as specialized pressure-vessel or code pipe welding. Our support is focused on contractor-directed field welding, structural and support steel, foundation-related steel, equipment support steel, repairs, modifications and project welding manpower.

EPC & Critical Infrastructure Welding FAQ

What is EPC welding support?

EPC welding support is field welding subcontractor support for engineering, procurement and construction contractors. Kings Mobile Welding supports EPC contractors with defined welding scopes, field modifications, structural steel welding, repair and reinforcement work, and project-based welding manpower. We do not act as the EPC contractor.

Do you provide welding support for EPC contractors?

Yes. Kings Mobile Welding provides field welding support for EPC contractors, general contractors, construction managers, industrial facilities, utility contractors and infrastructure teams that need welding manpower for project-specific scopes.

Do you provide welding services for critical infrastructure projects?

Yes. We support welding services for critical infrastructure such as transportation infrastructure, bridges, utility infrastructure, energy projects, industrial facilities, ports, marine construction, heavy civil work and deep foundation projects when the scope is contractor-directed and project requirements are provided.

Can you provide welding support for energy and utility projects?

Yes. Kings Mobile Welding can support defined field welding scopes for energy and utility construction involving structural steel, support steel, field modifications, repairs, punch-list welding, foundation-related steel and scheduled project welding.

Do you provide industrial site welding services?

Yes. We provide industrial site welding services for industrial construction, plant and facility work, structural steel, equipment support steel, field modifications, reinforcement, maintenance-related welding and shutdown project support.

Can Kings Mobile Welding work from engineered drawings and project specifications?

Yes. Kings Mobile Welding can perform contractor-directed field welding from drawings, project specifications, weld details, approved procedures and responsible-party direction. Engineering design and final approval should remain with the responsible engineer, owner, inspector, manufacturer or qualified authority.

Can you provide supplemental welding manpower for an active construction project?

Yes. We can provide project-based mobile welding support and supplemental field welding manpower for active construction projects, scheduled phases, multi-day scopes and contractor-directed welding packages.

What types of structural steel welding can you perform on EPC projects?

Relevant work can include field-welded structural steel connections, structural modifications, beam and column repair, steel joist reinforcement, embed plates, base plates, support steel, pile and deep foundation welding support, bridge and transportation infrastructure welding support, and industrial shutdown welding support.

What areas do you serve for EPC and infrastructure welding projects?

Kings Mobile Welding is based in Newport News and supports qualifying commercial, industrial, heavy civil and infrastructure projects throughout Virginia, North Carolina, Maryland, Washington DC, South Carolina and the Mid-Atlantic.

How do I request pricing for an EPC or infrastructure welding scope?

Send drawings, specifications, welding details, bid documents, project location, desired start date, schedule, manpower requirements and photos of existing conditions when applicable. We will review the scope and confirm pricing and availability.
