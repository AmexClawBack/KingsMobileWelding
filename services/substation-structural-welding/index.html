Substation Steel

Structural welding support for new work, expansion, and existing substations.

Kings Mobile Welding works as a specialty field-welding subcontractor supporting EPC contractors, electrical contractors, utility contractors, substation contractors, steel teams, and general contractors. We handle the structural and miscellaneous steel portion of the work — including installation support, field modifications, reinforcement, repairs, punch-list welding, and corrective work performed to the project drawings and applicable welding procedures.

Scope We Support

The structural steel around the electrical equipment.

Substation welding can range from new structural installation support to small field corrections that hold up energization, equipment setting, access, or closeout. Our focus stays on the steel.

01 / Equipment Support Steel

Frames, H-frames & equipment supports.

Field welding support for structural frames and steel supporting substation equipment where welded connections, attachments, modifications, reinforcement, or installation corrections are required.

Platforms, stairs, handrails & access steel.

Expansion & Brownfield Work

Existing substations rarely give you perfect field conditions.

Brownfield and expansion work often means existing steel, existing foundations, equipment already in place, tight access, revised layouts, and field dimensions that do not match the original assumptions. That is where a field-welding crew can keep the structural scope moving.

Expansion Projects Additional structural steel, equipment supports, platforms, framing, reinforcement, and tie-in work for substation expansion.
Brownfield Modifications Modify existing steel around new equipment, revised access, changed layouts, replacement components, or owner-directed upgrades.
Field-Fit Work Cut, fit, modify, and weld steel where actual field conditions require adjustment from the fabricated condition.
Reinforcement Add plates, stiffeners, support members, connection steel, or other reinforcement where shown on approved drawings or repair details.
Field Corrections & Punch List

When steel arrives wrong, does not fit, or needs to change.

Schedule pressure often shows up in the final field corrections. Kings can support contractor-approved modifications and corrective welding when structural steel is misfabricated, misaligned, incomplete, damaged, or changed after fabrication.

Where the correction requires an engineered detail, the responsible engineer, owner, EPC, or project design team provides or approves the repair. Kings performs the field work to that approved detail and applicable WPS requirements.

01
Misfabricated Steel Corrections

Field trimming, plate changes, attachment relocation, bracket modification, connection adjustments, and welding to approved correction details.

02
Punch-List Welding

Incomplete welds, missed attachments, support steel, tabs, brackets, miscellaneous metals, and closeout items that need a qualified field welder.

03
Field Modifications

Cutting, grinding, fitting, reinforcement, welding, and fabrication changes required to make the installed condition match the approved field direction.

04
Schedule Recovery

Supplemental welding support when a project team needs corrective structural work completed without pulling its own electrical or erection crews away from core work.

Scope Boundary

Structural welding — not electrical work.

Kings Scope Structural steel welding, field fabrication, support framing, reinforcement, modifications, repair welding, cutting, fit-up, and miscellaneous metals.
Electrical Scope Energized work, live-line work, switching, electrical terminations, grounding, cable installation, equipment commissioning, and other electrical responsibilities remain with the utility or electrical contractor.
Engineering Kings does not independently engineer or approve structural modifications. Engineered repairs and modifications are performed to owner-, engineer-, utility-, or EPC-approved details and applicable WPS requirements.

FAQ

Substation welding questions.

What types of substation steel can Kings work on?

We support structural and miscellaneous steel including equipment supports, H-frames, structural frames, platforms, brackets, access steel, stairs, handrails, base plates, secondary support framing, reinforcement, and project-directed field modifications.

Can you correct misfabricated or field-fit steel?

Yes, when the project team provides or approves the required field correction. We can perform cutting, fit-up, modification, reinforcement, and welding in accordance with approved drawings, correction details, and applicable WPS requirements.

Do you perform energized electrical or live-line work inside substations?

No. Our scope is structural and mechanical field welding and fabrication. Electrical work, energized work, switching, grounding, terminations, commissioning, and live-line responsibilities remain with the utility or electrical contractor.

Need structural welding support at a substation?

Send the structural drawings, project location, steel details, field photos, RFIs or punch-list items, welding requirements, access conditions, and schedule. We’ll review the scope and confirm field-welding availability.
