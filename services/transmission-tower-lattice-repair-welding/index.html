Lattice Structures

Field welding support for the steel members and connections that make up the tower.

Kings Mobile Welding supports transmission contractors, utility contractors, structure-repair companies, EPC teams, and other primes that need structural welding capacity for existing lattice towers. Our role is field execution: member replacement, reinforcement, weld repair, connection work, and contractor-directed modification performed to approved drawings, repair details, and applicable WPS requirements.

Repair Scope

Repair work tied directly to the tower structure.

Lattice structures distribute load through a network of legs, bracing, connection plates, gussets, and secondary members. When a repair is required, the field work has to follow the approved geometry, sequence, and connection detail.

01 / Members

Replacement & reinforcement members.

Where an approved repair calls for replacing, reinforcing, or supplementing a structural tower member, Kings can provide the field cutting, fit-up, alignment, and welding required to install the new steel in accordance with the issued detail.

  • Replacement structural members
  • Reinforcement members
  • Localized strengthening steel
  • Field-fit replacement sections
  • Member extensions or modifications
  • Approved connection changes
02 / Connections

Gusset plates, connection plates & attachment steel.

Connection repairs may involve new plates, reinforcing plates, replacement attachment steel, or localized weld work around existing joints. The exact weld type, length, sequence, and preparation are controlled by the project detail and WPS.

  • Gusset plates
  • Connection plates
  • Attachment plates
  • Reinforcement plates
  • Connection modifications
  • Localized weld repair
03 / Bracing

Bracing repair & structural strengthening.

Damaged, deficient, or modified bracing can require reinforcement or replacement to restore the structure to the approved repair condition. Kings can support the structural welding portion once the repair design and required access have been established.

  • Diagonal bracing
  • Horizontal bracing
  • Secondary members
  • Reinforcement steel
  • Structural strengthening details
  • Contractor-directed modifications
04 / Weld Remediation

Cracked, defective & deficient weld repairs.

When inspection identifies a weld condition that requires repair, Kings can perform weld removal, preparation, and rewelding in accordance with the approved repair procedure. Carbon arc gouging, grinding, cutting, and fit-up can be incorporated where the issued detail requires them.

  • Defective weld remediation
  • Crack removal where directed
  • Carbon arc gouging
  • Grinding and joint preparation
  • Repair welding
  • Inspection-ready cleanup
Field Repair Conditions

Existing towers add access, sequencing, and fit-up challenges.

Tower repair is rarely a shop-style welding environment. Existing steel geometry, elevation, right-of-way access, member loading, adjacent operations, weather, and inspection requirements all affect how the work is staged and executed.

Existing Geometry Member dimensions, connection locations, distortion, corrosion, and actual field conditions can affect fit-up and repair sequencing.
Replacement Steel New members, plates, bracing, or reinforcement need to match the approved material, dimensions, and connection detail.
Elevated Access Work-at-height planning, lifts, climbing systems, scaffold, rigging, or other access methods are coordinated around the project scope and controlling contractor.
Inspection Visual inspection, project hold points, owner review, and any required NDT are coordinated around the approved repair sequence.
Engineered Repair Execution

The repair design stays with the responsible engineer.

Kings Mobile Welding does not independently engineer or approve transmission tower repairs. The responsible engineer, utility, owner, or EPC provides or approves the repair detail, member requirements, connection configuration, welding requirements, and repair sequence.

Our role is to execute the structural welding scope in accordance with the approved repair package and applicable WPS. When requested, we can coordinate with welding engineering resources for project-specific WPS development.

01
Repair Detail

Issued tower repair drawing, member identification, connection detail, weld symbols, dimensions, and limits of work.

02
Material Confirmation

Existing member material, replacement steel, reinforcement plate grade, thickness, filler-metal requirements, and surface condition.

03
Repair Sequence

Member removal, temporary support or unloading requirements, fit-up sequence, welding sequence, and inspection hold points as directed by the project team.

04
WPS Compliance

Applicable process, low-hydrogen controls, preheat/interpass requirements, weld progression, environmental limits, and closeout requirements.

Field Coordination

The welding plan has to fit the tower-access plan.

Tower work is often controlled by elevation, site access, outage restrictions, rigging, weather, and the prime contractor's repair sequence. Those conditions should be reviewed before the welding crew mobilizes.

01

Elevated Work

Coordinate approved fall-protection and access methods with the controlling contractor before structural welding begins.

02

Right-of-Way Access

Plan welding machines, leads, generators, consumables, material handling, and crew access around remote or limited-access sites.

03

Rigging & Member Handling

Replacement members and repair steel may require contractor-provided rigging, lifting, stabilization, or positioning before fit-up and welding.

04

Outage / Work Windows

Structural repairs can be scheduled around approved outage periods, restricted work windows, night work, weekend work, or other project constraints.

Repair Workflow

From tower detail to completed field repair.

Send the repair drawings, tower identification, member information, field photos, access plan, project schedule, welding requirements, and inspection requirements before mobilization. The more complete the repair package is, the more accurately the field plan can be built.

01

Repair Package Review

Review tower drawings, member identification, repair details, dimensions, connection geometry, field photos, and project notes.

02

Qualification & WPS Review

Confirm applicable code, welder qualifications, base metal, filler metal, preheat/interpass requirements, inspection points, and documentation needs.

03

Access & Crew Planning

Coordinate crew size, welding equipment, work-at-height access, rigging interfaces, site access, hot-work requirements, and work windows.

04

Preparation & Fit-Up

Remove or prepare deficient weld areas, fit replacement steel, align members or plates, establish the approved joint condition, and prepare for inspection hold points.

05

Welding & Closeout

Complete the structural welding to the approved detail and WPS, support required inspection, finish final cleanup, and provide requested field documentation.

FAQ

Lattice tower repair questions.

Can Kings replace or reinforce lattice tower members?

Yes, when the project team provides or approves the repair detail, member requirements, connection configuration, welding requirements, and sequence. Kings can perform the field preparation, fit-up, and structural welding required by that approved repair.

Does Kings engineer transmission tower repairs?

No. Structural design and repair approval remain with the responsible engineer, owner, utility, or EPC. Kings executes the field welding in accordance with approved drawings, repair details, material requirements, and applicable WPS requirements.

Do you perform energized or live-line work on transmission towers?

No. Our scope is structural and mechanical field welding and fabrication. Electrical, energized, live-line, conductor, switching, grounding, and related utility responsibilities remain with the utility or electrical contractor.

Have a lattice tower repair detail to review?

Send the tower drawings, repair detail, member identification, replacement or reinforcement steel information, WPS requirements, access plan, inspection requirements, project location, and requested schedule. We’ll review the structural welding scope and confirm availability.
