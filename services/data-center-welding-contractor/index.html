Data Center Field Welding FAQ

Do you provide welding crews for data center construction projects?

Yes. Kings Mobile Welding can support data center and mission critical construction projects with structural field welding crews for defined scopes, miscellaneous metals, equipment support steel, field modifications, inspection repairs, punch-list welding and schedule-driven overflow manpower.

Does Kings Mobile Welding travel for data center work?

Yes. Kings Mobile Welding is based in Newport News, Virginia and can travel for qualifying data center and mission critical projects with appropriate scope, duration, onboarding requirements and schedule.

Can you provide a 2–3 person field welding crew?

Yes. Crew size depends on scope, schedule and availability, but small traveling field welding crews are a core target for this service. Send the project location, expected duration, welding scope and desired mobilization date for review.

What type of welding do you perform on data centers?

We focus on structural steel, field-welded connections, equipment support steel, miscellaneous metals, platforms, catwalks, stairs, ladders, handrails, guardrails, support framing, field modifications, inspection repairs and punch-list welding.

Do you perform data center piping or pipe welding?

No. Kings Mobile Welding's data center services are focused on structural steel, miscellaneous metals and field welding support. We do not provide process piping, mechanical piping, chilled-water piping or pipefitting services.

Can you supplement another steel contractor's crew?

Yes. We can work under another contractor as a welding subcontractor to supplement a steel erector, fabricator, miscellaneous metals contractor, general contractor or specialty contractor when the project needs additional field welding manpower.

Can you perform inspection repair and punch-list welding?

Yes. We can support contractor-directed inspection repair items, closeout welding, punch-list welding, structural steel corrections, added plates, stiffeners, bracing and field-fit modifications when project requirements are provided.

Do you perform equipment support steel welding?

Yes. We can support structural steel and miscellaneous steel associated with generator support steel, transformer support steel, UPS and switchgear support steel, electrical equipment framing, support frames, access platforms, catwalks, dunnage and equipment support framing.

Can you work from engineered drawings and project specifications?

Yes. We can perform contractor-directed field welding from engineered drawings, project specifications, approved details and responsible-party direction. Engineering design and final approval should remain with the responsible engineer, owner, inspector, manufacturer or qualified authority.

Do you provide overflow welding manpower?

Yes. Kings Mobile Welding can provide overflow welding support and supplemental welding manpower for schedule-driven field welding scopes, multi-week projects, punch-list phases, inspection repair work and project closeout scopes when availability and project requirements align.

What information should I send when requesting a traveling welding crew?

Send project location, GC or contracting company, project schedule, anticipated start date, expected duration, number of welders needed, drawings, welding details, specifications, photos, required welding processes, required certifications, work schedule, shifts and site-specific onboarding requirements.

Need a Traveling Field Welding Crew?

If your data center project needs additional structural welding manpower, send Kings Mobile Welding the project location, scope, schedule, anticipated duration and welding requirements. We can review whether a traveling crew is a good fit for the project.
