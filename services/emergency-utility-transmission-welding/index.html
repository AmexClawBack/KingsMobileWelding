Emergency Utility Welding

When the structural problem cannot wait for the normal project schedule.

Utility and transmission damage can create immediate pressure on the project team. A storm, vehicle strike, equipment impact, failed weld, damaged member, or discovered structural deficiency may require a welding crew on short notice. Kings Mobile Welding provides structural field-welding support once the repair direction, safe work conditions, access, and applicable welding requirements are established.

Emergency Scopes

Structural repair support for the conditions utilities actually encounter.

Emergency does not mean uncontrolled. The work still needs a safe site, a defined repair, proper materials, applicable procedures, and coordination with the utility or controlling contractor.

01 / Storm Damage

Storm-damaged utility and transmission structures.

High winds, falling trees, debris, flooding, and other storm-related events can damage structural steel around transmission and utility infrastructure. Once the damaged structure is stabilized and the repair detail is established, Kings can support the structural welding portion of the recovery work.

  • Damaged steel members
  • Reinforcement plates
  • Broken or failed welds
  • Connection repairs
  • Replacement steel
  • Structural strengthening
02 / Impact Damage

Vehicle strikes, equipment impact & localized structural damage.

Vehicle, crane, equipment, or construction-related impact can damage poles, frames, support steel, brackets, platforms, and other utility structures. Kings can execute engineer- or owner-approved reinforcement, replacement, or repair welding once the damaged area is safe and accessible.

  • Transmission pole damage
  • Substation structural steel
  • Support-frame damage
  • Deformed brackets and attachments
  • Connection damage
  • Localized reinforcement
03 / Failed Welds

Cracked, broken, deficient & incomplete welds.

Inspection or active operations may uncover a weld condition that requires immediate correction. Where the approved repair calls for weld removal and rewelding, Kings can provide carbon arc gouging, grinding, preparation, fit-up, and repair welding to the applicable procedure.

  • Cracked welds
  • Broken attachments
  • Incomplete structural welds
  • Carbon arc gouging
  • Repair-weld preparation
  • Inspection-ready closeout
04 / Outage & Critical Windows

Structural work that has to fit a narrow work window.

Some scopes are not emergency damage, but the available repair window is still critical. Kings can support structural welding during approved outage periods, shutdowns, overnight work, weekends, or other restricted windows when the project team needs dedicated welding capacity.

  • Outage-window welding
  • Night and weekend work
  • Schedule-recovery welding
  • Punch-list corrections
  • Late-stage field modifications
  • Short-duration mobilization
Before Mobilization

Fast response starts with the right information.

For an urgent repair, the first call should establish whether the welding crew can actually mobilize productively. Photos and a location are helpful, but the repair also needs enough technical direction to select the right equipment, consumables, materials, and crew.

Structure & Damage What structure is damaged, what failed, where the damage is located, and whether the structure has been stabilized or isolated as required.
Repair Direction Engineer, owner, utility, or EPC repair detail; field sketch; RFI response; weld symbols; member or plate requirements; and any repair sequence.
Welding Requirements Applicable code, WPS, base metal, filler metal, preheat/interpass requirements, inspection points, and documentation.
Site Readiness Access, excavation, lifts, rigging, safe work boundary, outage status, hot-work permit process, and who is controlling the work area.
Material Availability Whether replacement steel, reinforcement plates, members, brackets, or other repair material is already on site or needs to be sourced.
Emergency Does Not Change the Standard

The repair still has to be approved, controlled, and weldable.

Kings Mobile Welding does not independently engineer emergency transmission or utility repairs. Repair design and structural approval remain with the responsible engineer, utility, owner, EPC, or project design team.

Our role is to execute the structural welding once the work area is safe, the repair has been defined, the required materials and access are available, and the applicable WPS and inspection requirements are established.

01
Safe Work Area

The structure and site must be stabilized, isolated, de-energized, grounded, or otherwise controlled as required by the utility and controlling contractor before welding starts.

02
Approved Repair Direction

Use the engineer-, owner-, utility-, or EPC-approved repair detail, member replacement detail, reinforcement layout, or other issued direction.

03
Applicable WPS

Confirm base metal, repair material, filler metal, joint detail, preheat/interpass requirements, process controls, and any project-specific procedure requirements.

04
Inspection & Closeout

Coordinate owner, engineer, third-party, or contractor inspection requirements so the repair can be accepted without avoidable delays.

Emergency Workflow

Move quickly without skipping the information that controls the weld.

The fastest path to mobilization is a clear repair package and a ready work area. We can review the available information quickly, identify what is missing, and determine what would be required to put a welding crew on the project.

01

Initial Damage Review

Structure type, location, photos, damage description, immediate safety status, project contact, and target repair timeline.

02

Repair & WPS Review

Approved repair direction, material requirements, WPS, weld symbols, filler metal, preheat/interpass controls, inspection requirements, and any missing technical information.

03

Mobilization Planning

Crew size, welding machines, generators, leads, consumables, travel, access, lifts, rigging interfaces, work hours, and site controls.

04

Field Repair

Preparation, cutting, gouging, fit-up, reinforcement, member or plate installation, structural welding, and coordination with required inspection.

05

Return to Project Team

Complete final cleanup, requested documentation, inspection closeout, and turnover of the structural repair for restoration, coating, reassembly, or subsequent utility work.

FAQ

Emergency utility welding questions.

Can Kings respond to emergency utility welding scopes?

Yes, for qualifying short-notice structural welding scopes when the work area is safe, the repair direction has been established, and the required access, material, welding procedure, and project controls are available. Call 757-231-6809 for an urgent scope.

Does Kings engineer emergency transmission repairs?

No. Repair design and structural approval remain with the responsible engineer, utility, owner, EPC, or project design team. Kings executes the field welding to the approved repair detail and applicable WPS requirements.

Do you perform energized or live-line emergency work?

No. Our scope is structural and mechanical field welding and fabrication. Electrical, energized, live-line, conductor, switching, grounding, and related utility responsibilities remain with the utility or electrical contractor.

Have an urgent transmission or utility repair?

Call first, then send the damage photos, repair detail, structure information, material requirements, WPS, access conditions, inspection requirements, project location, and schedule so we can determine what is needed to mobilize.
