Pole Repair

When the structure stays in service, the repair has to match the detail.

Existing transmission poles may require reinforcement because of local buckling, impact damage, corrosion-related section loss, deficient welds, deformation, or other conditions identified through inspection. Kings Mobile Welding provides the field-welding crew to execute the approved repair — including surface preparation, plate fit-up, welding, reinforcement, and final cleanup — while the responsible engineer, utility, owner, or EPC retains design authority.

Repair Scope

Focused on structural repair and reinforcement.

Repair geometry varies by pole design and the condition being corrected. Our field work follows the issued repair detail, material requirements, weld symbols, approved sequence, and applicable welding procedure.

01 / Local Damage

Buckling, deformation & localized pole damage.

Localized shell deformation can occur near the pole base or along a shaft section. Where the engineer-approved repair calls for external reinforcement, welded plates, stiffening, or another structural modification, Kings can provide the field preparation, fit-up, and welding required to complete the repair.

  • Local shell buckling
  • Damaged pole flats
  • Base-area deformation
  • Impact-related structural damage
  • Localized section reinforcement
  • Engineer-directed strengthening
02 / Reinforcement

Plate reinforcement & external strengthening.

Reinforcement repairs may use individual plates, multi-plate arrangements, wrap-style details, or other engineer-specified steel placed around the existing pole geometry. Our crews handle field layout, surface preparation, fit-up, tack-up, welding, and cleanup around the issued repair design.

  • Reinforcement plates
  • One plate per pole flat
  • Multi-sided repair details
  • Full-perimeter plate welding
  • Localized structural strengthening
  • Field fit-up and alignment
03 / Weld Repair

Weld remediation & corrective field work.

Some transmission structure repairs involve correcting deficient welds, removing unacceptable weld metal, repairing attachment locations, or completing project-directed structural modifications. Carbon arc gouging, grinding, cutting, fit-up, and rewelding can be incorporated when they are included in the approved repair procedure.

  • Deficient weld removal
  • Carbon arc gouging
  • Crack excavation where directed
  • Repair welding
  • Attachment modification
  • Corrective punch-list work
Materials & Procedure Control

Weathering steel repairs require more than matching plate dimensions.

Existing transmission poles may be fabricated from ASTM A588 or other weathering steels, while the reinforcement material may be a different high-strength structural grade. The approved repair package and WPS should establish how the materials are to be joined, including filler metal, preheat, interpass temperature, weld size, sequence, and inspection requirements.

Existing Pole Material grade, wall thickness, pole geometry, coating or weathered surface condition, and repair location.
Repair Steel Plate or member grade, thickness, dimensions, fit-up tolerance, and placement around the existing structure.
WPS Applicable process, filler metal, electrode classification, low-hydrogen controls, preheat/interpass limits, and welding sequence.
Inspection Visual inspection, owner or third-party hold points, NDT requirements, documentation, and final acceptance criteria when specified.
Repair Responsibility

Kings executes the repair. The responsible design party approves it.

Kings Mobile Welding does not independently engineer or approve transmission pole repairs. The repair design is provided or approved by the responsible engineer, utility, owner, or EPC. We execute the structural welding scope in accordance with the approved repair drawings, material requirements, weld details, and applicable WPS.

If the project requires a procedure that is not already available, we can coordinate with welding engineering resources for project-specific WPS development when requested by the contracting team.

02

Field Execution

A repair sequence built around the issued detail.

The exact sequence is project-specific, but the field work typically moves from access and surface preparation through fit-up, controlled welding, inspection, and cleanup.

01

Access & Exposure

Confirm the repair area is accessible, excavated where required, and ready for welding equipment, leads, ventilation, and inspection access.

02

Surface Preparation

Remove loose corrosion products, coatings, contamination, or other material from the weld zone as required by the repair procedure.

03

Layout & Fit-Up

Position repair plates or members to the approved dimensions and tolerances, establish alignment, and prepare the joint for welding.

04

Preheat & WPS Controls

Apply required preheat and maintain procedure controls for electrode storage, interpass temperature, sequence, and environmental conditions.

05

Repair Welding

Complete the welds in accordance with the approved detail and WPS, including multi-pass or full-perimeter welds where specified.

06

Inspection & Cleanup

Support required inspection, complete final weld cleanup, remove temporary fit-up aids when applicable, and leave the repair area ready for the next trade or closeout.

FAQ

Steel pole repair questions.

Can Kings repair a buckled steel transmission pole?

We can perform the field welding for an engineer-, owner-, utility-, or EPC-approved repair detail. The responsible design party determines how the pole is to be repaired; Kings executes the welding scope to the approved detail and applicable WPS.

Can reinforcement plates be welded to an A588 weathering steel pole?

Yes, for qualifying repair scopes where the project has established the applicable base-metal, reinforcement-material, filler-metal, preheat, interpass, and WPS requirements. The repair package should identify those requirements before field welding begins.

Who handles excavation around the pole base?

Excavation, shoring, access, and restoration are normally coordinated by the prime, utility, owner, or site contractor unless those items are specifically included in our quoted scope. We need the repair area exposed and safely accessible before welding begins.

Have a steel pole repair detail to review?

Send the repair drawing, pole geometry, material information, reinforcement plate details, WPS requirements, access conditions, inspection requirements, and schedule. We’ll review the field-welding scope and confirm mobilization availability.
