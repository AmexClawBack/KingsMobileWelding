Transmission Structures

Field welding support for the steel carrying the power-delivery system.

Kings Mobile Welding supports transmission contractors, utility contractors, EPC teams, structure-repair companies, and project owners that need qualified welding capacity in the field. Our role is structural welding and field fabrication: reinforcement, repair, replacement members, weld remediation, and modifications performed to project drawings, approved repair details, and applicable welding procedure requirements.

Transmission-structure welding built around the field conditions.

Engineered Repair Execution

The repair detail comes first.

Kings Mobile Welding does not independently engineer or approve transmission structure repairs. Repair design is provided or approved by the responsible engineer, utility, owner, or EPC. We execute the welding scope in accordance with the approved drawings, repair details, material requirements, and applicable WPS.

When a project requires a procedure that is not already available, we can coordinate with welding engineering resources for project-specific WPS development when required by the contracting team.

Before Welding

  • Approved repair drawings and limits of work
  • Base metal and reinforcement material review
  • Weld symbols and joint requirements
  • Applicable WPS and welder qualifications
  • Preheat / interpass requirements when specified

Field Execution

  • Surface preparation and cleaning
  • Plate or member fit-up and alignment
  • Carbon arc gouging where required
  • Welding to approved procedure requirements
  • Inspection support and final cleanup
