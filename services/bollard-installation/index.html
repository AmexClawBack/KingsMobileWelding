Bollard Installation FAQ

Do you install bollards in Newport News, Hampton, Norfolk, Portsmouth, and Suffolk?

Yes. Kings Mobile Welding provides bollard installation in Newport News, Hampton, Norfolk, Portsmouth, Suffolk, Chesapeake, Virginia Beach, Williamsburg, and surrounding Hampton Roads areas.

Can you fabricate custom steel bollards?

Yes. We can fabricate custom steel pipe bollards, capped bollards, base plate bollards, chain bollards, bollards with welded eyelets, removable-style details, and owner-directed custom bollard layouts.

Can you paint or powder coat bollards safety yellow?

Yes. Bollards can be painted or powder coated safety yellow or another owner-selected color depending on the project requirements, finish expectations, quantity, and schedule.

Do you install bollards in concrete?

Yes. Bollards may be surface-mounted, core-drilled, embedded, or installed according to a contractor-directed or owner-directed detail. Concrete thickness, slab condition, rebar, underground utilities, and engineered requirements should be confirmed before installation.

Are your bollards crash-rated?

We can install contractor-provided or owner-specified bollards, but crash-rated, anti-ram, security-rated, or vehicle impact-rated bollards should be specified by the manufacturer, engineer, project documents, owner, or qualified authority. We do not label a bollard as crash-rated unless the system and installation are specified and documented that way.

What is the fastest way to get a bollard installation price?

Send the site address, photos, desired bollard quantity, height, diameter, layout, finish, access notes, schedule, and whether you need fabrication, delivery, installation, or all three. Start on the contact page.
